Belgium, regional layer: guidance on work authorisations (single permit, work permit B, professional cards)
Checked 2026-11-15. Collected by download_be_regions.py. Files are listed in index.tsv; what is known or presumed to exist but is not published or could not be read is in not-obtained.tsv. The federal circulars and the country-of-origin material in be/ are a separate collection and were not touched.

Since the sixth state reform (2014) the Flemish Region, the Walloon Region, the Brussels-Capital Region and the German-speaking Community decide on the admission of foreign workers to employment and on professional cards for the self-employed; the federal Immigration Office decides the residence side of the single permit.

VL  Flanders (Departement Werk, Economie, Wetenschap, Innovatie en Sociale Economie, Dienst Economische Migratie): nothing published for decision-makers. Read: https://www.vlaanderen.be/werken/een-buitenlander-in-vlaanderen-tewerkstellen and https://www.vlaanderen.be/werken/in-vlaanderen-komen-werken/beroepskaart-voor-buitenlandse-ondernemers with all 47 pages below them (categories, exemptions, wages, appeal, legislation, professional-card conditions, simulators, newsletter). They hold information for employers and applicants, application forms, model contracts, declarations, the yearly wage thresholds and the lists of occupations (not wanted), and links to the legal texts in the Vlaamse Codex (law of 30 April 1999, decree of the Flemish Government of 7 December 2018). No omzendbrief, ministerial circular or "richtlijnen" on the 2018 decree, the 2024 reform or the amending decree of 1 December 2025 is linked, and web search found none; the search hits are Flemish Government decision papers (themis.vlaanderen.be), SERV opinions and the service's annual report 2019-2022 (https://publicaties.vlaanderen.be/view-file/55645), none of which is guidance. All downloadable files of the site sit on assets.vlaanderen.be, whose robots.txt excludes everything (User-agent: * / Disallow: /); none was fetched, and none of those listed is guidance.

WAL Wallonia (SPW Emploi-Formation, Direction de l'Emploi et des Permis de travail): two pages saved. Read: https://emploi.wallonie.be/home/travailleurs-etrangers.html and the 61 pages below it (permis de travail, permis unique, the categories including the EU Blue Card, carte professionnelle, au pair). Almost all of it is information for employers and workers, forms, infographics and links to the legal texts (Walloon decrees of 16 May 2019 and 6 June 2024, law of 19 February 1965). Two pages are written for those who handle professional-card applications on the Region's behalf: "Guichet d'entreprises" (the accredited business counters) and "Poste diplomatique" (Belgian diplomatic and consular posts). They say which residence documents make an application admissible, what to verify, how to forward the file and how to deliver the card, and they state the service's practice on some points (e.g. applications for platform work are refused). No circular or instruction on the decrees of 2019/2024 is published.

BRU Brussels-Capital (Brussels Economy and Employment, Direction de la migration économique): site not read. robots.txt of economie-emploi.brussels (the same file serves economie-werk.brussels and economy-employment.brussels) shuts out AI agents by name (anthropic-ai, ClaudeBot, Claude-Web, GPTBot, CCBot and others: Disallow: /), so no page was requested. Web search (the site's pages on permis de travail and cartes professionnelles) and the Wayback Machine's index of the site's PDF files (https://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=economie-emploi.brussels/sites/default/files*, same for economie-werk.brussels) show application forms, GDPR notices and applicant pages only. No instruction or circular on the ordinance of 1 February 2024 or the Government decree of 16 May 2024 is known to be published. Not verified on the site itself.

DG  German-speaking Community (Ministerium der Deutschsprachigen Gemeinschaft, Fachbereich Beschäftigung): nothing published for decision-makers. Read: https://ostbelgienlive.be/desktopdefault.aspx/tabid-269 (Arbeitserlaubnis) and https://ostbelgienlive.be/desktopdefault.aspx/tabid-4759 (Berufskarte): applicant information and two application forms. The sub-pages on the single permit and the unlimited work permit seen in web search are applicant information too.

How much real guidance: practically none. The four authorities publish their rules as decrees and explain them to employers and applicants on their websites; the instructions their case officers work from are not public. The two Walloon pages are the only published texts addressed to bodies that process applications, and they concern the reception and forwarding of professional-card files, not the assessment on the merits. Anyone needing the internal instructions would have to ask under the regional access-to-documents rules.

Refusals and exclusions met: assets.vlaanderen.be (robots.txt: everything excluded), economie-emploi.brussels (robots.txt: AI agents excluded). No HTTP 403/429 and no bot check was met.

EU Blue Card and agency work (added 15 November 2026, second session): neither saved document deals with the EU Blue Card or with employment through temporary work agencies or employers of record. The Blue Card is explained only in applicant pages (Wallonia's category pages, the Flemish "categorieën" pages); none of the pages read in the four regions mentions interim/uitzendarbeid for third-country workers. No further sources were found in this session; the Brussels and Flemish asset exclusions above still apply.
